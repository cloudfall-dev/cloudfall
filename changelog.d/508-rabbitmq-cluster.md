### Added

- `services.yml` converges a RabbitMQ service of three nodes (S-023): each node runs node discovery (4369) and the inter-node port (25672) on its server's IPv4, which its firewall admits from the other two nodes only, resolves every node's hostname to its IPv4 through a managed block of `/etc/hosts`, and speaks TLS between nodes, with the command-line tools, using its own certificate from the project CA (usage server and client, at `/etc/rabbitmq/cloudfall/`, the key made on the node and readable by `rabbitmq` alone) verified against that CA in both directions. The service's home converges first and the other nodes join it one at a time; once every declared node is a member, the role waits up to an hour until the service is healthy (every node a running member, no alarm, no partition, every quorum queue with a leader and a member online on every node) and otherwise stops with what is missing. Adding two nodes to a service of one grows it the same way, with no queue redeclared: continuous membership reconciliation grows each quorum queue to three members. Before it changes or restarts anything, the role reads the cluster and, when no more than half of its members run, stops the converge with `PRECONDITION` (`service_rabbitmq_no_majority`) and restarts no node. `inspect` reads a cluster's members on its home, and `audit` reports a declared node that is not a running member and a cluster without majority (#508)
- A RabbitMQ service of three nodes may declare `clientAccess`: each node takes clients over TLS on its own IPv4 (5671), admitted from `allowedSources` only, with the same certificate from the project CA it meets the other nodes with (#508)
