### Added

- A change of a RabbitMQ service's `version` to a later patch of its series, or to the next accepted series, converges as a rolling change instead of being refused (S-023): from the service's home, which `services.yml` runs first, every other node in declared order and the home last, one at a time. Before each node stops, the cluster must be healthy (every member running, no alarm, every quorum queue with its members online), `rabbitmq-upgrade await_online_quorum_plus_one` must find that every quorum queue keeps its majority without it, and `rabbitmq-upgrade drain` moves its clients and queue leaders to the other nodes; every stable feature flag is enabled before the first node stops. Each node's `rabbitmq-server` is released from its hold, installed at the new version and held again, started, revived, and the cluster must be healthy again before the next one. After the home, every stable feature flag is enabled and every member must report the declared version. A service of one node changes with a short stop and no quorum wait. An older `version` than one a node runs, and a series before the installed one or past the next accepted one, are refused before any task changes a host; a node other than the home refuses a version change of its own (#509)
