### Added

- A `Service` can be `serviceKind: rabbitmq` (S-023): `spec.rabbitmq` declares `version` (an exact `X.Y.Z` of a series Team RabbitMQ still supports for the open-source edition, `4.3` or `4.4`, held in one table with each series' end of community support and its Erlang range, 27.0 or later), `memoryHighWatermarkMb` (512 or more), and optionally `nodes` (one server or three, containing `spec.server`), `vhosts` (each with an `owner` and a `passwordRef`, never a password) and `clientAccess` with its `allowedSources`. `cloudfall config validate` refuses an unsupported version (`service_rabbitmq_version`), `nodes` without the service's server, with an undeclared or repeated server, or of another size than one or three (`service_rabbitmq_nodes`), a server that is a node of two RabbitMQ services (`service_rabbitmq_server_shared`), a node of another environment (`service_rabbitmq_environment`), two nodes of one hostname (`service_rabbitmq_hostname`), `remoteAccess` (`service_rabbitmq_remote_access`), a virtual host without an owner or `passwordRef`, with an `application`, declared twice, or whose owner owns another (`service_rabbitmq_vhost`), the owner `guest`, the virtual host `/` and names starting `cloudfall_` (`service_rabbitmq_reserved`), with three nodes or `clientAccess` a server without a firewall (`service_rabbitmq_unfirewalled`) or an IPv4 (`service_rabbitmq_server_unaddressed`), a client source of every address (the S-006 rule), and a server without one storage pool, mirrored for one node (the S-014 codes); it warns `RABBITMQ_VERSION_ENDING` with the date when a series is within 90 days of its end of community support, and `RABBITMQ_SINGLE_NODE` for one node in the `production` environment. The rendered inventory carries the service, with `nodes` resolved and its virtual hosts' password references, never a password, on each of its nodes; nothing converges it yet (#505)
