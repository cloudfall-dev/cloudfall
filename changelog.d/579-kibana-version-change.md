### Added

- Kibana follows a change of its cluster's version (S-024-9): changing the Elasticsearch service's `version` to a later patch or minor moves Kibana in the same converge, after every node. Before Kibana's held package changes, the `cloudfall_kibana` role reads every node's version (`GET /_nodes`, `no_log`) and, while one is older than the declared version, leaves Kibana as it is and stops the converge, which answers `PRECONDITION` with the code `service_kibana_node_older` and the nodes still behind. Kibana then restarts on the new version and the converge waits until it reports itself available, its saved objects migrated. A converge that finds Kibana at the declared version reads nothing more; a newer Kibana, or one of another major, is still refused before anything changes (#579)
