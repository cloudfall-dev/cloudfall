### Added

- A `Service` can be `serviceKind: elasticsearch` (S-012): `spec.elasticsearch` declares `version` (an exact `X.Y.Z` of a line Elastic still supports, `8.19` or `9.x`), `heapMb` (512 to 31744), and optionally `nodes`, `clusterName`, `existingCluster`, `users` (each with `indexPatterns` and a `passwordRef`, never a password), `remoteAccess`, `snapshotRepository` and `restoreCheckIndices`. `cloudfall config validate` refuses an unsupported version (`service_elasticsearch_version`), bad `nodes` (`service_elasticsearch_nodes`), an `existingCluster` without `clusterName`, with a CA file that is missing, outside the project, not a CA or holding a key, or with a seed host that is a declared node (`service_elasticsearch_existing_cluster`), a server in two clusters (`service_elasticsearch_server_shared`), several nodes or an existing cluster without a `snapshotRepository` (`service_elasticsearch_snapshot_repository_local`), a reserved or repeated user name (`service_elasticsearch_user_reserved`, `service_elasticsearch_user_duplicate`), a pattern of every or a hidden index (`service_elasticsearch_pattern`), and `remoteAccess` under the PostgreSQL rules; it warns `ELASTICSEARCH_VERSION_ENDING` with the date when a line is within 90 days of its end of life. The rendered inventory carries the service, with `nodes` and `clusterName` resolved, on its server; nothing converges it yet (#309)
