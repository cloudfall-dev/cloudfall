### Added

- `services.yml` converges a ClickHouse service of one replica with no `clientAccess` (S-022) through the new `cloudfall_clickhouse` role: `clickhouse-server`, `clickhouse-client` and `clickhouse-common-static` at the declared `version` from the `lts` suite of ClickHouse's APT repository, all held; the data directory, Keeper's logs and snapshots included, on a `clickhouse` volume (20 GB, mirrored for one replica) that the converge decides with the other volumes; the `cloudfall` password Cloudfall generates (`cloudfall secrets generate`), kept on the replica as the `0600` client configuration `/etc/clickhouse-server/cloudfall-client.xml` and in `users.d` as its SHA-256 alone. Managed settings in `config.d/9*-cloudfall-*.xml` (clients on `127.0.0.1` only, at `bind.port` and 8123; `max_server_memory_usage` from `maxServerMemoryMb`; Keeper inside the server with its one voter on loopback; the replica named for its server and the shard for the service; users, roles and grants made by SQL kept in Keeper) and `users.d/90-cloudfall-users.xml` (the `default` user removed, `cloudfall` from loopback only). The role refuses, before it changes the host, a `maxServerMemoryMb` above nine tenths of the server's memory (so does `services.yml` before any service role), a series it does not install, a version other than the installed one, and two replicas or `clientAccess`, which come later; after the start it refuses a server that reports another version, and a ClickHouse or Keeper listener outside loopback. On the service's server it creates each declared database with the `Replicated` engine and its owner, who logs in from loopback with the salted SHA-256 the controller computed from its `passwordRef` (the password never reaches the server, and a second converge changes nothing), has every privilege on its own database and none elsewhere, and a settings profile that admits replicated tables only; a declared database that exists with another engine is refused. A database Cloudfall made that is no longer declared is kept with its rows and its owner can no longer log in; `inspect` reads the service's databases and users, and `audit` lists those as undeclared and anything Cloudfall did not make as unmanaged (#568)
