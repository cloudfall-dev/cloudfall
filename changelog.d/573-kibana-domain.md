### Added

- A Kibana service with `kibana.domain` converges published through that `Domain` (S-024-8): `services.yml` hands the `cloudfall_kibana` role the Domain from its server's own Domains, and the role writes `server.publicBaseUrl: https://<primaryName>` and `xpack.security.secureCookies: true` to `kibana.yml`, so the links Kibana generates start with that address and the session cookie is sent over HTTPS only. Kibana keeps listening on loopback; the Domain's certificate and nginx site in front of it are the ones `cloudfall domains apply` already manages. The role no longer refuses a declared `domain`; it refuses, before any change, one its server does not hold proxying to the Kibana port with `tls.mode: required` and no `routes`. A Kibana without `domain` is unchanged (#573)
