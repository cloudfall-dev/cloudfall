### Added

- A `Service` can be `serviceKind: kibana` (S-024): `spec.kibana` declares `elasticsearch` (the id of the Elasticsearch service Kibana reads, of the same environment, with `spec.server` one of its nodes), `heapMb` (1024 to 8192), and optionally `domain` (the `Domain` that publishes it) and `users` (users of that Elasticsearch service, each with `access` of `view` or `edit`). The kind declares no `backup`, which stays required for every other kind. `cloudfall config validate` refuses `backup`, `metrics` or `remoteAccess` on the kind (`service_kibana_field_refused`), an `elasticsearch` that names no Elasticsearch service (`resource_reference_missing`) or one of another environment (`service_kibana_environment`), a server that is not one of its nodes (`service_kibana_server_not_node`), an Elasticsearch service that declares `existingCluster` (`service_kibana_existing_cluster`), a second Kibana for one Elasticsearch service or on one server (`service_kibana_duplicate`), a `heapMb` outside its range (`service_kibana_heap`), a user the Elasticsearch service does not declare or named twice (`service_kibana_user_unknown`), and a `domain` that names no Domain (`resource_reference_missing`) or one whose `proxy.server`, upstream or `tls.mode` does not publish this Kibana, or that declares `routes` (`service_kibana_domain`); it warns `KIBANA_PUBLISHED` when `domain` is declared. An Elasticsearch user may no longer be named `cloudfall-kibana-view` or `cloudfall-kibana-edit` (`service_elasticsearch_user_reserved`), the roles the Kibana role will keep. `cloudfall backup run` and `backup verify` refuse a Kibana service with `PRECONDITION` (`lifecycle_service_no_backup`) before reaching any server, naming the Elasticsearch service whose snapshots hold its saved objects. The rendered inventory carries the service on its server, beside the Elasticsearch service it reads; `services.yml` still refuses the kind until its role lands (#567)
