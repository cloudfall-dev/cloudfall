### Fixed

- `decisions approve` no longer settles a decision `verified` when its verify step observed no host: every host the check run or the approved run left recap counts for must have counts from the run and a task the verify step ran on it, and a verify play that matched no host, skipped a targeted one or skipped every task on it settles the decision `failed`, with a verdict naming the hosts that were not observed. Before, a verify step that exited 0 and listed no host as changed proved the run, even when it looked at nothing. A verify playbook aimed at other hosts than the run's (such as `localhost` checking a URL) now settles `failed` too; aim it at the hosts the operation targets (#360)
