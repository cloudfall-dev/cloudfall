### Added

- An `OperatorPolicy` grant may name the operation classes of a project's server-changing MCP tools, `deploy`, `rollback`, `restart`, `migrate`, `data-copy`, `domains-apply` and `converge-domains`, beside `converge-services` and `converge-baseline`; the `OperatorProposal` schema gains, additively, trigger kind `agent` with its `request`, `operation.servers` and `operation.environments`, approval mode `agent` with `policy`, `grant` and `client`, and `binding`, for the agent records spec 018 writes. Over `cloudfall mcp serve` in a project, the plan of `deploy`, `rollback`, `restart`, `migrate`, `data_copy` and `domains_apply` called without `yes` gains `approval.selfConfirm` and, per environment its servers are in, the `policy` that applies or none and the `reason` from the operator's grant rules; nothing a confirmed call does changes yet (#474)
