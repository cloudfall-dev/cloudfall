### Changed

- `migrate --yes` checks a `deploy:<component>` step's approval as `deploy --yes` does: for an application that declares `approval: manual` and no approval that counts, it pauses at that step with `PARTIAL_FAILURE`, `context.code: deployment_approval_missing`, the built `release` and the `cloudfall release approve` `command`, and once a person approved it, `migrate --yes` resumes there with the release the completed `build:<component>` step recorded, without building again and without needing `--build` again. The deploy step's detail gains `approval` (the record it went on), `migrate` gains `--approvals` (default `decisions/releases`, bound by `cloudfall mcp serve`) and the `RECORD_INVALID` exit for an approvals file that isn't a valid `ReleaseApproval`. `rollback` needs no approval: its plan gains `approval`, `{"policy": "manual", "required": false, "reason": ...}` for a `manual` application and `{"policy": "automatic"}` otherwise (#165)
