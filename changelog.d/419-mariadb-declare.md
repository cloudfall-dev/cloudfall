### Added

- A `Service` can be `serviceKind: mariadb` (S-019): `spec.mariadb` declares `version` (an exact `X.Y.Z` of a long-term series MariaDB still maintains: `10.11`, `11.4`, `11.8` or `12.3`), `innodbBufferPoolMb` (128 or more), and optionally `databases` (each with an `application`, or an `owner` with a `passwordRef`, never a password, and `authentication: ed25519` if wanted), `remoteAccess`, and `replication` (`role` `primary` or `replica`, `serverId`, a primary's `listenAddress`, a replica's `primary` service or `source` with `host`, `port`, `credentialsRef` and `caCertificateFile`). `cloudfall config validate` refuses an unsupported version (`service_mariadb_version`), a server with two MariaDB services (`service_mariadb_server_shared`), a reserved owner or database name (`service_database_owner_reserved`), a repeated `serverId` (`service_mariadb_server_id_duplicate`), a primary without `listenAddress` or a replica with one (`service_mariadb_replication_listen`), a replica without exactly one `primary` or `source`, or whose `primary` is no primary MariaDB service of its environment on another server (`service_mariadb_replication_primary`), a replica with `databases` (`service_mariadb_replica_databases`), of another series than its primary or older (`service_mariadb_replica_version`), a pair without a firewall (`service_mariadb_replication_unfirewalled`), a replica server with no address its primary can admit (`service_mariadb_replica_unaddressed`), a source CA file that is missing, outside the project or not a CA (`service_mariadb_replication_source`), a server without one mirrored storage pool (the S-014 codes), and `remoteAccess` under the PostgreSQL rules; it warns `MARIADB_VERSION_ENDING` with the date when a series is within 90 days of its end of life, and `MARIADB_REPLICATION_UNENCRYPTED` for a source without a CA file. The kind yields its `mariadb` volume, package, replication port, certificate and generated replication password as host requirements, and the rendered inventory carries the service with its databases and references, never a password; nothing converges it yet (#419)
