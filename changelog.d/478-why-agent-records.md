### Added

- `cloudfall why` now answers the agent records and agent proposals of the proposal store beside the decisions: the run a policy grant let a confirmed `cloudfall mcp serve` call make, and the proposal a call no grant covered left for a person. A new `--proposals` flag names the store (default `record/operator/proposals`). `--host` matches any server in the record's `operation.servers`, `--status` its status and `--since`/`--until` its call, run and verify times; `--operation` and `--stale` ask about decisions only. Each such answer carries `agent_record` (the tool, the class, the approver: `agent` with its client or `human` with how it arrived, the policy and grant when there is one, the reason, `plan_sha256` and the status), the record itself under `proposal`, and a story in which the agent's reason is escaped so a terminal prints it as the characters it is; `--format html` draws it as a card. An agent record that cannot be read is skipped with a `RECORD_UNREADABLE` warning, as an unreadable decision is. The operator's own alert and drift proposals are not part of the answer, and the answer for decisions alone is unchanged (#478)
