### Added

- `cloudfall audit` checks a Kibana service (S-024-13): `kibana.version` (the installed `kibana` package against the Elasticsearch service's declared `version`), `kibana.unit` (`kibana.service` enabled and running), `kibana.configuration` (`/etc/kibana/kibana.yml` against the rendered settings, with a setting the role does not write named without its value, and the heap in `node.options`), `kibana.status` (the overall level of `GET /api/status` is `available`), `kibana.roles` (`cloudfall-kibana-view` and `cloudfall-kibana-edit` as the role writes them), `kibana.users` (each declared user of the Elasticsearch service holds the Kibana role of its `access` and no other; an undeclared user holding one is listed as unmanaged, not drift) and `kibana.keystore` (the three encryption keys, its S-011 secret requirements, by setting name). A Kibana listening off loopback is drift of the existing `services.bind` check. Inspect reads the evidence on the Kibana server as the `cloudfall` account in `no_log` tasks and keeps names and privileges only: no token, key, password or hash reaches the observation. A project without Kibana audits as before. `docs/kibana-service-guide.md` now covers declaring Kibana and its users, an SSH forward, publishing with a Domain and what that exposes, a version change, the licence and the Kibana features that need a paid one, and the audit (#582)
