### Added

- A `Service` can be `serviceKind: clickhouse` (S-022): `spec.clickhouse` declares `version` (an exact `X.Y.Z.N` of a long-term support series ClickHouse still maintains, `26.3` or `26.8`, held in one table with each series' end-of-support date), `maxServerMemoryMb` (2048 or more), and optionally `replicas` (one server or two, containing `spec.server`), `keeper.servers` (one voter for one replica, three for two, containing every replica), `databases` (each with an `owner` and a `passwordRef`, never a password) and `clientAccess` with its `allowedSources`. `cloudfall config validate` refuses an unsupported version (`service_clickhouse_version`), `replicas` without the service's server, with an undeclared or repeated server, or of more than two (`service_clickhouse_replicas`), `keeper.servers` that misses a replica, names an undeclared or repeated server, or holds another number of voters (`service_clickhouse_keeper_servers`), a server that is a replica or voter of two ClickHouse services (`service_clickhouse_server_shared`), a server of another environment (`service_clickhouse_environment`), `remoteAccess` (`service_clickhouse_remote_access`), a database without an owner or `passwordRef`, with an `application`, or whose owner owns another (`service_clickhouse_database`), the names `default`, `cloudfall`, `system`, `information_schema`, `INFORMATION_SCHEMA` and names starting `cloudfall_` (`service_database_owner_reserved`), with two replicas or `clientAccess` a server without a firewall (`service_clickhouse_unfirewalled`) or an IPv4 (`service_clickhouse_server_unaddressed`), a client source of every address (the S-006 rule), and a server without one storage pool, mirrored for one replica (the S-014 codes); it warns `CLICKHOUSE_VERSION_ENDING` with the date when a series is within 90 days of its end of support, and `CLICKHOUSE_SINGLE_REPLICA` for one replica in the `production` environment. The rendered inventory carries the service, with `replicas` and `keeper.servers` resolved and its databases' password references, never a password, on each replica and voter; nothing converges it yet (#566)
